Your music. A more predictable exit.

Development notes · Work recorded on November 17, 2026.

Where things stand

FML’s published private-test update focuses on local listening and predictable playback. Swiping it away from Android Recent apps stops playback and closes the playback service; using Home allows background listening.

Help shape the next session

For testers, the useful distinction is leaving the app versus closing it. Try both during an ordinary listening session and describe whether the result matches what you expected. Feedback on finding music and reading the player controls is welcome too.

What still needs proving

The skin-unlock test is explicitly free and simulated. It is not a real Google payment and does not establish production billing or restoration after reinstalling. The published record still leaves physical-device swipe-away acceptance pending. Test builds remain private.
