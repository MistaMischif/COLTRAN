Development notes · Work recorded on November 16, 2026.

Where things stand

King of the Cinder connects a vehicle-combat race, an on-foot crossing of the Killing Fields and a climb to the Power Tower. The public development story records work on car selection, crosshair alignment and clearer weapon effects, with creator playtests reaching summit victory.

Help shape the next session

The feedback we want is about the journey: is the next objective obvious when you leave the car, can you follow incoming fire, and does the transition into the climb keep the pace moving? Describe the phase and what happened before the confusing moment.

What still needs proving

The published record leaves two-person synchronization, contested summit defense and larger multiplayer sessions needing further verification. A configured player limit is not proof of a full-capacity playtest. This update does not claim those checks have since passed.
