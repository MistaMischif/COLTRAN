Before you join

Testing, clearly explained.

How do I install the test?

Request access by email. Once you are enrolled and the test is available, you will receive the Google Play opt-in link and installation instructions by email. Use the same Google account for enrollment and Google Play. You do not need ChatGPT.

Do skins cost money during the private test?

No. The private build uses a labeled “Test purchase — Free” flow. It simulates unlocking and restoring skins locally, with no Google checkout or charge. It does not verify real Google Play billing, and test unlocks do not become paid purchases.

What is the planned release price?

The base player and Classic Journey remain free. Each of the other 23 skins is planned at US $1.99, with local pricing in supported markets. Commercial purchases are separate from the free private-test flow.

Does FML include music or stem separation?

Bring your own audio files; FML does not include a commercial music catalog. The current private build does not provide stem separation. DJ mode uses playback transitions, not separated vocals or instruments.

What feedback helps?

Tell us your phone model, Android version and what happened. Try local playback, DJ transitions, free test purchases and restore, and swiping the app away from Recent apps. Keep your original music files backed up.
