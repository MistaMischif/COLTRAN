Private development testing · Ages 13+ · Access coordinated individually

One squad. New ways to survive.

C2G is growing from its top-down roots into a standalone Quest experience. Build defenses from the parts you earn, choose equipment and decide where your squad's next shot matters most.

Mobile & browser

Squad loadouts, inventory, crafting and progression. The latest recorded browser build improves mole emergence animations and prevents moles attacking before they reach the visible first attack row.

Browser build 102. Offline HTML can run without a server; online features use the hosted game service.

Standalone Quest preview

Try the first ten levels. Assign a different character to each hand, aim independently and switch between top-down and immersive views inside the same Quest battle.

Quest preview 9. Development build; temporary sprite artwork remains alongside 3D content.

A friend on mobile. You in VR.

Our self-hosted Get Help co-op prototype lets one mobile player join a Quest-hosted battle and control an available character through a browser. We're looking for pairs who can help test the connection, controls and feel of playing together.

Linked browser and Quest devices can also synchronize campaign progress between rounds. Joining and sharing are still being refined; seamless no-pause invitations and Quest-native friends/invites are not available in this preview.

Playtesters wanted

Help us make the next round better.

First-time players and experienced testers are welcome. We want honest feedback on aiming, crafting, difficulty, readability and VR comfort—and what makes you want one more round.

Request a private test slot

No email app? Send C2G playtest request to info@coltranstudios.com, our current studio contact for these requests.

Include your preferred platform, device model and whether you'd like to test solo or with a friend. Confirm you're 13 or older; no date of birth or ID is needed. We'll coordinate access individually. A request does not guarantee an immediate slot.

Your email and supplied details are used to respond and coordinate testing. Do not send passwords or recovery codes. See our privacy policy.
