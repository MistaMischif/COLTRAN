Cradle to the Grave
Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy covers Cradle to the Grave (C2G), including its browser, mobile and Meta Quest versions and COLTRAN-operated game services. The person responsible is Jason Zielinski, operating as COLTRAN Studios, United States.

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, email privacy@coltranstudios.com.

Information used to run the game

  • Local play: your device stores settings, inventory and campaign progress. VR controller and headset input is used to render and control gameplay.
  • Optional online features: our game server stores a randomly assigned player ID, chosen display name, friendships, invitations, scores and run results. Linked devices can store and synchronize campaign progress. Session credentials, recovery-code hashes and temporary pairing codes support access and recovery.
  • Cooperative battles: the server relays battle state, display names and aim/fire commands between the host and guest. The other participant can see the shared battle. A guest does not receive the host's account credentials.
  • Connection information: servers process IP addresses and request information to deliver pages and game traffic, limit abuse and diagnose connection problems.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include.

The current game service does not collect your address book or use advertising networks or third-party analytics. We do not sell player information. The current game does not record microphone audio or camera video.

Meta Quest platform features

Meta's SDK supports checking whether your account is entitled to use the Quest app. Meta also processes information under its own policies.

Quest friends and invitations are in development and are not enabled in the current Alpha 9 release. Subject to Meta approval and feature availability, C2G will use your app-scoped Meta user ID and profile information to identify you in the game, available app-related friends information to show people you can invite, and invitation/deep-link destination data to open the intended game or battle. Invitations will be initiated by the player. We do not use this information for advertising or sell it.

What others can see

Your chosen display name and submitted scores can appear on friends and public leaderboards. Friends can see relevant friend and challenge information. Anyone you give a battle invitation to may use it to request access while it is valid. Avoid using sensitive information as your display name and share invitation links only with intended recipients.

Service providers

The studio website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Privacy email is forwarded by ImprovMX to a Gmail inbox; these providers process the message and delivery information. Optional public test connections may pass through Cloudflare when a test tunnel is enabled. These services can process connection information needed to provide their services. The game server itself supports self-hosted operation. Meta operates the Quest platform and its platform APIs.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law. We do not authorize providers to use game data for our advertising. This policy covers COLTRAN-operated services; an independently operated self-hosted server has its own operator and practices.

Storage and retention

Local saves remain on your device until removed or reset. Online profile, progress and score records are retained to provide your account and requested game features until deleted. Invitations expire, and temporary pairing codes expire after five minutes. Cooperative rooms are temporary, expire after inactivity and are cleared when the server restarts. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve requests and maintain necessary records. Deletion may take additional time to reach backups; information retained for legal obligations is restricted to that purpose.

Services and providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. We use access controls and protected session credentials, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You can play available offline modes without using online social or synchronization features. You can remove friends and stop sharing a battle. Clearing local app/browser storage removes local saves but does not by itself delete server records.

To request access, correction or deletion, email privacy@coltranstudios.com with the subject C2G privacy request, your display name and which version or service you used. We may ask for reasonable verification so we do not alter another player's information. Never email passwords, recovery codes, session tokens or payment information. We will explain a safe verification method if needed.

Deleting an online profile removes its active server records, including associated scores, friendships, invitations, sessions and synchronized progress. Copies on your own devices must also be removed there. You may have additional privacy rights under the law where you live; contact us to exercise them.

Age requirement

C2G is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the game or website as appropriate.
